# Step skein-14.s1: the kit's finds across a page's own navigation

Ruling 126, in session skein 14, before 14.2. A find whose root
(`DOM.getDocument`'s) was read before the page navigated by itself
reaches Chromium 147's renderer after the new document loads, and the
renderer crashes (`logs/skein-14/14.2-ruling-125-diagnosis.md`, run 4).
It starts from skein 14's WIP on `skein-ui/skein-14-2-restart`: its kit
part (116 lines in `browser.rs`, one in `limits.rs`) and the regression
test become this step's commit; the restart part stays 14.2's.

Files: `testing/skein-browser/src/{browser.rs, limits.rs}` and their
  host tests; `tests/browser/tests/browser_real.rs`
  (`chromium_survives_a_page_that_reloads_itself`, its JavaScript and
  Wasm pages) and the Rig's scenario in `tests/browser/tests/support/real.rs`.
Shape: no find sends Chromium a root read in an earlier document. The
  WIP holds DOM and accessibility reads while the page navigates, drops
  a find's pending reads at a navigation, and reads a fresh root after
  the load event; an `Await` keeps its deadline. What it leaves is a
  race under load; the diagnosis below names it. A candidate, which the
  diagnosis decides: a root Chromium checks against the document, the
  `objectId` of `document` from `Runtime.evaluate` in the page's current
  context, refused with an error once that context is gone, in place of
  `DOM.getDocument`'s `nodeId`; the find retries at its next poll.
Diagnosis first: at most 4 `heavy` commands and 25 minutes. Each runs
  the gate's browser line (`scripts/check.sh:31`) 5 times, after the
  gate's build lines, with run 4's buffered trace; it reports the
  crashes counted, and one crashing run's trace.
Test shape: the WIP's three host tests of the hold; the regression,
  about 1 s.
Proof: live, one `heavy` command: the gate's browser line 5 times, no
  `Crash`; then `heavy --gate scripts/check.sh` once.
Don't: accept `Crash` in any test, retry a crashed page, or slow the
  polls to dodge the race. A find that does not ask the role and the
  name of CDP (browser.md 6: "exactly, both asked of CDP"), such as
  `Accessibility.getFullAXTree` filtered in the kit, is a question for
  the designer: stop.
Size: aim for at most 160 lines of production code; past 220, stop and
  report the shape.
