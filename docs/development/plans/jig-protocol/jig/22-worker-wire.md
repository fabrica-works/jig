# Session 22: `jig-protocol-worker-wire`, the channel's records and sizes

A new codec crate gives jig's channel between an engine and its workers
its schema: the magic, one version, fifteen kinds of records carrying
jig's `domain/hosts.md`, section 2, field for field as jig 05's link
vocabulary holds it, the worker's credential, jig's refusal reasons, and
the function from the domains' limits to each kind's largest body and
each side's output cap. Its records are generated by skein's
`skein-codegen` from a schema and committed, with skein's drift test and
fuzzed decoders in a small world of their own, `tests/worker-wire`. Both
sides of the channel (jig 23, jig 24) use it; nothing in jig speaks the
channel today.

## What the design asks

- **skein's channel underneath** (channel.md, 1 and 4.1): "jig gives it
  a schema: its magic, its one version, its kinds". The worker is the
  initiator, the engine the responder.
- **The records, by their fields** (channel.md, 5.1 and 5.2): up, hello,
  turn, call, answer, fact, rejected, exhausted; down, assignment,
  message, relayed, turn kept, turn busy, acknowledge, cancel, grant.
  "Refusals ride with turns": a turn and an answer carry "at most as many
  as the run's inbox takes".
- **Bytes of other owners** (section 6): smith's charter, turns, call
  names and results; the application's items, left work, saved work and
  evidence; host tools' inputs. Carried, never decoded here.
- **A fact carries jig's activity, never smith's bytes** (6.4).
- **The worker's credential** (4.1): "the worker's name ...; its secret;
  the application's payload version".
- **jig's reasons, from 256** (4.1): *unauthorized*, *application*,
  *busy*, *replaced*, *not admitted*, *stopping*.
- **Grants name their table** (section 8): "A grant names whose table
  its value comes from: jig's accounts' or the application's"; validity
  "crosses as a duration".
- **A credential is a `secret N`** (skein's `codec.md`, section 3): "Its
  bytes cross as `bytes N` would; its type, lib's `Secret` ..., shows
  only its length in `Debug`".
- **Sizes** (section 11): "a plain struct of numbers ... The crate turns
  them into each kind's largest body and each side's output cap. It
  depends on no domain." The caps are section 9's sums.

## Choices this plan makes

- **The magic is `jigw`; `VERSION` is 1;** the family is
  `jig-worker-wire 1`, in `schema/worker-wire-v1.schema`.
- **Kinds:** up `0x0100` to `0x0106` (hello, turn, call, answer, fact,
  rejected, exhausted), down `0x0180` to `0x0187` (assignment, message,
  relayed, turn kept, turn busy, acknowledge, cancel, grant); every kind
  required, as smith's are.
- **Reasons:** 256 *unauthorized*, 257 *application*, 258 *busy*, 259
  *replaced*, 260 *not admitted*, 261 *stopping*.
- **Widths:** task, attempt and a message's name `u64`, as the core
  numbers them (`skein_lib::Token`'s raw value); a turn's number `u32`;
  spend `u64`; an LLM account and an application's credential `u32`; a
  generation `u64`; the payload version `u32`; a text's retry and offset
  `u32`.
- **The schema changes within the plan at version 1:** nothing outside
  this plan reads the channel (README, "Rules"; `protocol/README.md`,
  section 7, "Pre-release"). 22.2 and 22.3 add kinds to it with their
  goldens; `VERSION` stays 1.
- **Names avoid the boundary's words:** no record, field or enumeration
  value is named with a word of `tests/boundary`'s list, and no
  identifier ends in `V<digit>` or `_v<digit>` (the kind table is
  `KINDS`, never `V1`), since the generated file is scanned like
  hand-written code (findings/protocol.md, 5.3).
- **The world is `tests/worker-wire`** (package `jig-worker-wire-world`,
  ordinary Rust): the drift test, the goldens decoded and the fuzzed
  decoders. The channel's protocol world, `tests/channel`, stays jig
  25's (code-organization.md, section 2).

## Rules that bind it

- "**Generated codecs** are committed under each codec crate's
  `src/generated/`, made by skein's generator from the crate's
  `schema/*.schema`, with skein's drift test, as `smith-transcript`
  does. Generated files do not count against size limits."
  (code-organization.md, section 2)
- "No increment of this plan ... adds a field or predicate that its
  counterpart across a boundary lacks" (code-organization.md, section 6):
  each record's fields are those of jig 05's vocabulary that it carries,
  plus the bytes that vocabulary names by token, and nothing else.
- "No increment of this plan ... adds a test that checks several
  behaviours under one name" (code-organization.md, section 6).

### 22.4 Sizes
